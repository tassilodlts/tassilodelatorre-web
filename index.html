Journeys under human power, told in photographs and film.

I've had folding boats since I was twelve. Last summer I paddled a 1950s folding kayak 2,580 km down the Danube, from Ulm to the Black Sea, with Fluffo as crew.

I trained as an architect at the TU München, I work as a model, and I've spent years in product photography. On every journey I shoot with the best camera gear I can carry.

The camera is how I bring the journey home: not postcards, but a story you can feel. I speak five languages and I've raced and paddled the coasts of southern Spain, Corsica and the Mediterranean all my life.
