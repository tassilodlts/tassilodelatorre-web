About

The camera is how I bring the journey home.

I’ve had folding boats since I was twelve. Last summer I paddled a 1950s folding kayak 2,580 km down the Danube, from Ulm to the Black Sea, with my dog Fluffo as crew. I trained as an architect, I work as a model, and I’ve spent years in product photography. The camera is how I bring the journey home.

Five languages, and a lifetime racing and paddling the coasts of southern Spain, Corsica and the Mediterranean.
