Questions

The practical part.

Which files can I drop in?

GPX (tracks and routes) and GeoJSON, which covers essentially every watch, bike computer and planning app. FIT and KML I convert by hand for now — send them to me by email and I will deal with them. Files with several tracks or segments are drawn as one journey.

Is my track uploaded anywhere?

No. The studio reads the file inside your browser; it never leaves your machine and there is no account and no database. If you order something, you send me the file yourself, by email, and I keep it only as long as the job takes.

Why is the free download only 2000 px?

Because a browser cannot render terrain and satellite imagery at print resolution, and because a screenshot is not a print. When you order, I re-render the same framing at 300 dpi on my own machine, grade it, and set the type by hand.

How long does it take?

Prints: five to ten working days from confirmation, plus shipping. Pages: about a week. Films: ten working days. During an expedition of my own it can be slower, and I will tell you before you pay rather than after.

Can I use the poster commercially?

The free download is for personal use — social posts, your own blog, printing at home. Commercial use, sponsor decks, advertising and anything a brand pays for is covered by the Page, Film and Brand products, which come with the rights written down.

What about the imagery and the elevation data?

The studio preview uses Esri World Imagery and the open Terrain Tiles elevation dataset, credited on the map. Anything I deliver commercially is rendered from imagery I hold a commercial licence for, so what you buy is clean to use.

Where does the tree come from?

Every print plants one native tree, planted by hand in the highlands of Guadalajara, Spain, and you get the coordinates. The whole story is here.
